Our Story

Founded in 2018, ChillPops began with a simple idea: create the most delicious, all-natural popsicles using only the finest ingredients. What started as a small cart in local farmers' markets has grown into a beloved premium dessert brand across the country.

The ChillPops Promise

Every ChillPop is handcrafted in distinct, small batches using fresh, locally-sourced fruits and premium ingredients. We never use artificial flavors, colors, or preservatives – just pure, natural goodness wrapped in a frosty, premium experience.
